## Current Focus

My work centers on AI systems that operate under real constraints: deterministic orchestration around non-deterministic model calls, retrieval with provenance, access control for sensitive data, and automations that decline or escalate rather than guess. I find financial technology a particularly interesting place to apply this because the cost of a confidently wrong answer is real money. I care about building things that run, that fail loudly rather than silently, and that a customer can actually trust.
